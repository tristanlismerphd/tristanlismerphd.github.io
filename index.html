01 / QUANTUM FOUNDATIONS · STATISTICAL MODELLING · HPC

GPT Rank Sweep — Dimensionality of Quantum State Spaces

A data-driven test of Born’s rule: does a 4-slit interference dataset require the full n²−1 = 15 dimensional state space quantum mechanics predicts? Built a cross-validated bilinear ALS fitting pipeline processing 1296-file experimental datasets, distributed across Compute Canada via SLURM. No assumed model — the data determines the answer.

02 / EXPERIMENTAL QUANTUM OPTICS · PHOTONICS

4-Slit Photonic Interferometer — Testing Born’s Rule

Designed and built a precision 4-rail photonic interferometer to measure the Sorkin parameter I⊂3⊂ — a direct test of whether quantum mechanics produces higher-order interference terms. Operating at the single-photon level with an emICCD camera, phase controllers, and 1024×1024-pixel detection. 16 shutter configurations × 81 phase combinations per dataset.

03 / QUANTUM FOUNDATIONS · THEORY

Trichotomy Theorem — Classification of Probabilistic Theories

Proved a classification theorem for Generalised Probabilistic Theories (GPTs): a complete characterisation of how operational theories can assign probabilities to measurement outcomes. The result constrains what physical theories — beyond quantum mechanics — are self-consistent. Reasoning about abstract physical systems at this level translates directly to knowing when experimental evidence is actually decisive.
