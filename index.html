Strava + Garmin Training Analytics

End-to-end data science project ingesting heart rate streams from the Strava and Garmin Connect APIs. GPT Rank Sweep — Dimensionality of Quantum State Spaces

Built a cross-validated bilinear ALS fitting pipeline that processes 1296-file experimental datasets, distributes jobs across Compute Canada via SLURM, and infers the dimensionality of quantum state spaces from raw photon-count data.
