Tristan Lismer

PhD Candidate · Institute for Quantum Computing, University of Waterloo

I work on experimental quantum optics, testing the foundations of quantum theory through photonic systems. My main project investigates higher-order quantum interference using multi-slit configurations and single-photon coincidence detection.

Outside the lab I compete in Ironman triathlons and ultra-trail running.
